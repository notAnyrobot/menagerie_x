# Unitree rubber-hand source evidence

These files are source snapshots, not runnable Astro models. Their original
mesh paths are relative to their upstream repositories. The maintained native
Astro variants and runtime assets are at the family root and `assets/`.
See `../rubber_hands.json` and the hand-adoption investigation for revisions,
hashes, inertial records, and the explicit Astro sample mounting assumptions.
Unitree material retains the family-root `LICENSE.Unitree` terms; ProtoMotions
source material retains `LICENSE.ProtoMotions` terms and `NOTICE.ProtoMotions`. The upstream Menagerie
G1 snapshot retains its `LICENSE.MuJoCo-Menagerie-G1` notice.

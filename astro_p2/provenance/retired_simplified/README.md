# Retired Astro P2 simplified collision variant

Retired on 2026-10-31 after the maintainer approved the collision design in
[`astro_p2.xml`](../../astro_p2.xml). Use that model and
[`scene.xml`](../../scene.xml) for the maintained fixed-hand configuration.

This folder preserves the final simplified model, scene, previews and
derivation record as frozen source evidence. They are excluded from the
active catalog, gallery, and native runtime acceptance tests. Their original
relative paths are intentionally unchanged inside the files; they are not
maintained runnable entry points in this location. No shared meshes were
removed. [snapshots.json](snapshots.json) records original paths and hashes.

Astro robot material remains [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE). The historical
scene's adapted Menagerie contact selection retains its
[BSD-3-Clause notice](../../LICENSE.Menagerie).
